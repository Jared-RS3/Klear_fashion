PROJECT OVERVIEW

The Vision

The proposed platform will allow users to upload photographs of themselves and virtually try on clothing using artificial intelligence.

Users will be able to upload clothing they own, build a digital wardrobe, create outfit combinations and save generated looks.

Future phases may introduce community profiles, influencer wardrobes, retailer catalogues, shoppable outfits, affiliate links and ecommerce integrations.

CORE PLATFORM CAPABILITIES

  1. 01AI-powered virtual clothing try-on
  2. 02User profiles and photo onboarding
  3. 03Personal digital wardrobes
  4. 04Clothing uploads and categorisation
  5. 05Saved outfits and generation history
  6. 06Community and social discovery
  7. 07Influencer profiles and collections
  8. 08Retailer product catalogues
  9. 09Shoppable outfits and affiliate tracking
→

The platform will be developed in controlled phases to validate the artificial-intelligence experience, reduce technical risk and maintain control over the development scope.

WHY AI TRY-ON IS THE MAIN COST — AND WHY THAT IS NORMAL

It is real compute, per image.

Every try-on renders a brand-new photorealistic image on the provider’s GPUs. That is what makes virtual try-on feel like magic — and, like every AI product, it is metered per generation, not a cheap database lookup.

It is billed by usage, on the client’s account.

AI generation is billed by usage and paid on the client’s own account. The applicable rate is confirmed as part of the final agreement, based on this proposal.

It grows only because the app is used.

Zero users, near-zero cost. A large AI bill is a symptom of success — high engagement — and is designed to be covered by a credit or subscription model, not absorbed as a loss.

SCOPE LIMITATIONS

AI and Product Limitations

Artificial-intelligence virtual try-on is intended initially as a visualisation and styling tool. It does not guarantee that a garment will physically fit the user in the same way as the generated image.

AI RESULTS MAY BE AFFECTED BY

  • Photograph quality
  • Body position
  • Lighting
  • Garment-image quality
  • Transparent fabrics
  • Layered clothing
  • Oversized garments
  • Complex patterns
  • Logos and text
  • Fabric movement
  • Missing side or rear product images

EXCLUDED UNLESS SPECIFICALLY QUOTED

  • Guaranteed sizing accuracy
  • 3D body scanning
  • Real-time video try-on
  • Full 3D avatar creation
  • Retailer integrations
  • Marketplace payments
  • Influencer payouts
  • Product delivery and returns
  • International tax management
  • Customer-support staffing
  • Legal services
  • Brand development
  • Marketing campaigns
  • Photography
  • Content production
  • Unlimited revisions
  • Unlimited AI generations
  • Unlimited maintenance
  • Third-party subscriptions
